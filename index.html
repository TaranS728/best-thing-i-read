The Best Thing I Have Read

One of the best things I have read lately is a novel by Emily Henry called "People We Meet on Vacation". The story of this novel follows Poppy and Alex, two people who meet at college, and through certain circumstances they end up slowly becoming friends despite their differences. They travel together every summer over the span of twelve years and evetually end up falling in love, not without some turmoil of course.

The relationship between the two main characters and how it developed throughout the book was why I enjoyed it the most. Their friendship felt very natural, and the slow burn of them revealing their feelings to each other over time was a riveting concept that kept me engaged. The format of the story also jumped between present day and every past summer vacation which was a very unique storytelling structure that I have never seen before, but was very welcoming.

What really made the book memorable was that it was more than just romance, it also focused on friendship, growing up, relationships, and figuring out what love truly means. It was invigorating right from the start and kept me interested until the very end.
